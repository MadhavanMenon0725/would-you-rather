# **App Name**: Chaos Bound

## Core Features:

- Chaotic Scenario Engine: A card-based interface presenting two randomized, ridiculous 'Would You Rather' options for immediate player interaction.
- The Chaos Meter: A glowing bar that increases based on how unpredictable the user's choices are compared to average datasets.
- Fake Stat Simulator: Post-selection display showing humorously high or low percentage splits of how others supposedly 'voted'.
- Generative Reaction Narrator Tool: An AI feature that uses tool reasoning to generate unique, snarky, and internet-meme inspired responses tailored to the specific choice just made by the user.
- Persona Finalist: AI-driven logic that synthesizes 10 choices into a final 'Chaos Title' (e.g., Unstable Genius) with a personalized descriptive roast.
- Rapid Progress Pipeline: A top-mounted visual tracker ensuring players know exactly where they are in their 10-question session.
- Session Controls: Interactive 'Play Again' and 'Share Result' buttons that allow users to restart the chaos or broadcast their results.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color is Neon Violet (#E566FF), providing a high-vibrancy pop against a dark canvas.
- Background color is a deep Shadow Obsidian (#161217), giving the app a late-night, internet-subculture feel.
- Accent color is Electric Indigo (#666BFF), used for secondary UI elements to maintain an analogous, modern aesthetic.
- Headline and card font: 'Space Grotesk' (Sans-serif) for its scientific yet tech-savvy aesthetic; Body text font: 'Inter' (Sans-serif) for high legibility on mobile screens.
- Large-format centered card layout optimized for mobile thumbs, with high vertical padding and depth-inducing shadows.
- Fluid 'Motion Blur' transitions between question sets and spring-loaded hover effects for rounded action buttons.
- Glitch-art style iconography to represent the chaos meter levels and UI buttons.
